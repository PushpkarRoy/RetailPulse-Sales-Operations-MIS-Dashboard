## 📌 Project Overview

This project is a high-impact **MIS Dashboard** built to solve a major operational crisis: **High Gross Sales vs. Low Net Profit**. By analyzing 10,000+ rows of transactional data, I identified a massive **67% revenue leakage** caused by returns and cancellations. 

> **"This dashboard was designed to stop 'Data Manipulation' and provide management with the 'Net Truth' of the business."**

---

## 🎯 Objectives

- **Reconcile Revenue:** Bridge the gap between 12.07 Cr Gross and 3.95 Cr Real Sales.
- **Isolate Red Flags:** Identify why the Return Rate (34.22%) is higher than the success rate.
- **Branch Benchmarking:** Compare city efficiency (Indore vs. Mumbai/Delhi/Bhopal).
- **Product Audit:** Pinpoint high-return products like Jeans and Electronics.
- **Seasonal Strategy:** Identify peak months (June, July, Dec) for inventory planning.

## 📈 Key Findings & Insights

### 💰 The Revenue Reality
- **Gross Revenue:** ₹12.07 Cr reported in raw data.
- **Realized Revenue:** Only **₹3.95 Cr** (Delivered orders only).
- **Profit Leakage:** Reported profit was 1.52 Cr, but **Real Profit is only 49.43 Lakhs**.
- **The Red Flag:** 75% of products are either cancelled or returned.

### 🏙️ City Performance
- **Indore & Bhopal** lead in total sales volume.
- **Efficiency Winner:** **Indore** is the only city where the "Delivered" count is higher than "Returned + Cancelled" counts.

### 👥 Operational Metrics
- **Delivery Success Rate:** Only **33%**.
- **Return Rate:** **34.22%** — This is the biggest threat to sustainability.
- **Average Delivery Time:** 4.0 Days across all regions.

### 🏷️ Product Performance
- **Revenue Leaders:** Rice, Headphones, and Laptops.
- **High-Risk Items:** **Jeans, Headphones, and Laptops** show the highest return rates.
- **Market Fit:** Mumbai is best for Electronics, while Indore dominates Clothing.

## 💡 Business Recommendations

1. **📦 Audit Quality Control:** Immediately review the "Jeans" and "Headphones" supply chain due to excessive returns.
2. **📍 Scale the Indore Model:** Apply Indore’s logistics and fulfillment process to the Mumbai and Delhi branches.
3. **🏷️ Dynamic Stocking:** Increase inventory for **Electronics in Mumbai** and **Grocery in Delhi/Bhopal** during June and December.
4. **⚠️ Stop Gross Reporting:** Shift all management KPIs to "Net Delivered" metrics to prevent misleading revenue figures.
